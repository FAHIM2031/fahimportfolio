Behind the models

Finally, meet the engineer behind the models — a quick peek into my world.

I’m based at Jahangirnagar University in Savar, Dhaka — and most days, you’ll find me somewhere between a dataset and a deployment.

I am a Machine Learning Engineer. I build and train ML / DL models and fine-tune pre-trained models. That means the unglamorous, important work too: cleaning data, engineering features, building proper pipelines, and checking that a model still makes sense outside a notebook.

My repositories tell the story better than I can: CNNs and RNNs next to NLP and sentiment analysis, an IoT anomaly detector with a live dashboard, an earthquake risk tool for geologists, a quiz generator that turns photos into notes and audio, and full-stack apps for fitness, mental health and education. If it helps people learn, stay healthy, or stay safe — I’m interested.

Outside the training loop, I’m still building: hackathon projects, course projects, and small experiments that sometimes grow into the next real thing in the garden above.
